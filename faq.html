Surgical Specialties

Orthopedic surgery covers selected conditions affecting bones, joints, ligaments and related structures. Services may include fracture surgery, joint replacement and ligament-related procedures.

Fracture surgery is used for selected broken bones where surgical treatment may be required. The treatment approach depends on the location, type and severity of the fracture.

General surgery may include procedures for conditions such as hernia, appendix, gallbladder, breast conditions and other commonly treated surgical problems.

Laparoscopic surgery is a minimally invasive surgical approach that uses small incisions and specialized instruments. It may be suitable for selected abdominal and general surgical procedures.

Gallbladder surgery may be recommended for selected patients with symptomatic gallstones or other gallbladder conditions. Your surgeon will assess your symptoms and investigations before recommending treatment.

Appendix surgery, also known as appendectomy, involves surgical removal of the appendix when clinically indicated. Suspected appendicitis requires timely medical assessment.

Oncology surgery involves surgical management of selected tumors and cancer-related conditions. Treatment planning depends on the type, location and clinical characteristics of the condition.

Obstetrics and gynecology surgical care includes evaluation and treatment planning for selected women's health conditions, including procedures such as hysterectomy when clinically appropriate.

Treatment & Recovery

Recovery varies depending on the type of procedure, individual health and the surgical approach. Your surgeon will provide specific recovery instructions after treatment.
