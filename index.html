    Frequently Asked Questions

    Find answers to common questions about consultations, surgery, hospital care and recovery.

    You can request a consultation through the Niva Surgery website or contact our team directly. Our team will help you choose the appropriate surgical specialist and available appointment time.

    Surgery is recommended only after a proper clinical evaluation. Your surgeon will review your symptoms, medical history and required investigations before discussing whether surgery is appropriate.

    Your surgeon will discuss your health concerns, examine your condition and review relevant reports. You can also ask questions about treatment options, surgery and recovery.

    Your surgical team will provide personalised instructions based on your procedure. These may include investigations, medicines, fasting instructions and admission details. Always follow the instructions provided by your doctor.

    Hospital stay depends on the type of surgery, your overall health and your recovery. Your surgeon will explain the expected hospital stay during treatment planning.

    Your care team will monitor you after surgery and provide appropriate pain management based on your procedure and individual needs. You will also receive instructions for recovery at home.

    Recovery time varies depending on the surgery and the individual patient. Your surgeon will guide you about physical activity, work, exercise and other daily activities during follow-up.

    Yes. Follow-up appointments help your surgeon monitor healing and recovery. Your team will tell you when you should return and what symptoms or concerns you should watch for.

    Surgery costs can vary depending on the procedure, treatment plan, hospital stay and individual requirements. Our team can provide information about expected treatment costs after your consultation.

    Absolutely. We encourage patients and their families to ask questions and discuss their concerns before making treatment decisions. Our team will explain the available options and next steps clearly.
