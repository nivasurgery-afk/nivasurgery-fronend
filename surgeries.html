SURGICAL INFORMATION

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our surgical specialties, procedures, treatment planning and recovery.

Fracture surgery involves surgical treatment of selected broken bones, including fractures of the wrist, ankle and long bones. Your orthopaedic surgeon will assess the fracture and recommend the appropriate treatment.

Orthopedic surgery may include procedures for joints, bones, ligaments and spine-related conditions. Treatment options can include joint replacement, ligament repair and other surgical procedures depending on the patient's condition.

Oncology surgery involves the surgical management of tumors and selected cancer-related conditions. Treatment planning is based on the type and location of the condition and the patient's overall clinical assessment.

General surgery may include procedures such as hernia repair, appendectomy, biopsy and other commonly performed surgical procedures. The recommended procedure depends on the patient's condition and clinical evaluation.

Obstetrics and gynecology surgical care includes evaluation and treatment of selected women's health conditions. Procedures may include hysterectomy and other specialist gynecological surgeries based on individual clinical needs.

Hernia surgery is performed to treat selected abdominal wall hernias. Your surgeon will evaluate the type, location and symptoms of the hernia before recommending the appropriate treatment approach.

Piles may require specialist evaluation when symptoms are persistent, troublesome or do not improve with appropriate conservative treatment. Your surgeon will assess the condition and discuss suitable treatment options.

Gallbladder surgery is used for selected gallbladder conditions, including symptomatic gallstones. A surgeon will review your symptoms and investigations before recommending treatment.

Varicose veins are enlarged veins that may be associated with leg discomfort and other symptoms. A specialist can evaluate your symptoms and determine whether further treatment or intervention is appropriate.

A new or persistent breast lump, unexplained breast changes or other concerning symptoms should be evaluated by a qualified healthcare professional. Further investigations or surgical treatment may be recommended depending on the findings.

Gynecomastia surgery is a procedure used to treat enlarged male breast tissue when surgical treatment is clinically appropriate. A surgeon will assess the condition before discussing treatment options.

Appendix surgery, also known as appendectomy, involves removal of the appendix when surgical treatment is indicated. Suspected appendicitis requires timely medical assessment.

Laparoscopic surgery uses small incisions and specialized instruments for selected surgical procedures. It may be used for certain abdominal and general surgical conditions when clinically suitable.

Surgical Care at Niva Surgery

Niva Surgery provides planned surgical care across orthopaedic, general surgery, oncology, women's health, proctology and vascular specialties. Patients receive consultation, evaluation, treatment planning and recovery-focused support.

Orthopaedic & Fracture Surgery

Our orthopaedic surgical services include fracture surgery, orthopaedic procedures, joint-related surgery, ligament repair and selected bone, joint and spine conditions. Treatment is planned according to the patient's condition and clinical requirements.

General & Laparoscopic Surgery

Niva Surgery provides surgical evaluation for hernia, appendix, gallbladder, breast and other general surgical conditions. Selected procedures may be performed using minimally invasive laparoscopic techniques when clinically appropriate.

Specialised Surgical Care

Our surgical network also covers oncology surgery, obstetrics and gynecology, piles surgery, varicose veins and gynecomastia surgery. Each patient receives specialist evaluation and a treatment plan based on their individual needs.
